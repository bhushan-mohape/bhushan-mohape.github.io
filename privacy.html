Privacy Policy
Sneaky Props: Hide and Seek

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Developer: Sneaky Props Games · Contact: bhushanm1010@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the mobile game Sneaky Props: Hide and Seek (the "Game") uses and why. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, levels, stages, unlocked items, settings and the player name you choose) is saved only on your device. We do not have accounts, logins or a database, and we cannot see this data. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Playing with friends

Same Wi-Fi rooms connect phones directly on your local network. No data leaves your network.

Online rooms pass game messages (your chosen player name, character choices, movements, actions and quick-chat phrases) between the phones in the room through our relay server, hosted by Cloudflare. These messages are forwarded in real time and are not stored by us. Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the connection (see Cloudflare's privacy policy). There is no free-text chat; quick-chat uses fixed phrases only.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google > Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose when to watch one.

4. In-app purchases

If the Game offers purchases (such as "Remove Ads"), payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the ad and hosting services described above, we do not share information with anyone.

7. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game. Online-room messages are relayed and immediately discarded. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to its policies.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: bhushanm1010@gmail.com
